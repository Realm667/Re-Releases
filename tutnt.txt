* Requirements and Getting Started *

Game                    : Doom II (DOOM2.WAD required; not included)
Source Port             : UZDoom 5.0.1 (tested target)
Renderer                : Hardware rendering with shaders and dynamic lights

Load tutnt.pk3 with UZDoom and select Doom II as the base game, or launch:

    uzdoom.exe -iwad DOOM2.WAD -file tutnt.pk3

Choose an episode from New Game. Starting with Episode 1 leads through the
main campaign; "Fury of Fire" is a separate episode.

Use the Reforged options menu for visual presets, effect quality, HUD scaling,
subtitles, comfort settings and class-ability controls. Lower visual quality
or reduce weather, reflections and postprocessing if performance is limited.
Default class-ability keys are Q (offense) and G (defense); existing bindings
are preserved and both actions can be remapped in the controls menu.

Widescreen and ultrawide presentation are supported. The old v1.x instructions
to disable shaders or restrict play to 4:3 no longer apply. Historical Skulltag
compatibility refers to v1.x; use the tested UZDoom target for this edition.

* What's New in v2.0 *

- Reworked HUD, boss health bars, mission objectives, pickup notifications,
  automap information and navigation cues.
- Three player classes with six active abilities, smoother recoil, adjustable
  movement tilt and surface-dependent footsteps.
- Secondary attacks for the Super Shotgun, Rocket Launcher, Plasma Rifle and
  BFG, including bouncing grenades with a tumbling voxel model.
- Cinematic title presentation, illustrated chapter reading, revised menus,
  campaign results and animated ending credits.
- English, German, Spanish and French text, scalable subtitles, louder remaster
  narration and an option to use the original voice recordings.
- Reworked skies, dynamic rain and snow, visor weather, wet surfaces,
  footprints, liquid effects, lava, smoke, steam, fire and ritual portals.
- Surface relief, selective brightmaps, CRT effects, expanded materials,
  natural terrain edges, voxel objects and updated vegetation.
- Shared visual and attack feedback for The Source in both campaign endings.
- Campaign scripting, checkpoints, cooperative respawns and cutscene fixes,
  plus effect quality controls and performance improvements.

The maintained feature overview and linked technical notes are available in
_docs/utnt/WHATS_NEW.md in the source repository. That overview distinguishes
implemented features from unfinished local work.
