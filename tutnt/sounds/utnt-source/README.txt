Source finale: Implosion Near + Implosion Far

Original sound effects by Klerrp, released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0):
Implosion Near: https://freesound.org/people/Klerrp/sounds/121942/
Implosion Far:  https://freesound.org/people/Klerrp/sounds/121941/
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This seven-second derivative combines reversed suction, both forward impacts,
and short reflections. Source audio was decoded from the public high-quality
MP3 previews; these are not the authenticated original WAV downloads.
The repository includes source WAVs, exact provenance and a reproducible mixer
under tools/audio/utnt-source/ and tools/build_source_audio.py.
